About us

A sweet atelier with alittle extra spice

Savor by Sree is a Dublin-based artisan sweet atelier, where creativity meets indulgence. From handcrafted cakes and decadent desserts to chunky cookies and savoury delights, we bring together timeless classics and globally inspired flavours with a modern twist.

Everything is made fresh to order, in small batches, so your bakes are made for you and not for a shelf.

Our journey. Savor by Sree began as a small home kitchen with a simple idea: take the desserts people love and give them the flavours we grew up with. From our first market stall to Taste of Dublin and our regular Saturday spot at Meeting House Square, every bake has been made by hand and shared with people who became regulars, and then friends.
